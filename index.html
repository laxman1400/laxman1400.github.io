From a targeting brief to one defensible activation state per customer.

Audience engineering is not just writing a WHERE clause. The real work is translating business language into deterministic rules, resolving overlaps and edge cases, enforcing consent and suppression, and handing production a dataset whose counts and behavior can be explained.

Join the minimum trustworthy sources needed to answer the targeting question.

Decision logicEligibility, exclusions, priority, dedupe

Make overlapping rules explicit and enforce the intended record grain.

Quality gatesCounts, freshness, consent, edge cases

Recurring AutomationSFMCSQLData Freshness

A trigger is a production system, not a one-time audience pull.

Daily, weekly, and monthly triggers require repeatable qualification windows, predictable refresh behavior, campaign-history awareness, and failure handling. The goal is to make the right customer become eligible at the right time - and stay out when they should.

CadenceDaily · Weekly · Monthly

Design selection windows and run schedules around the business event.

StateHistory + eligibility + frequency rules

Use prior activation and customer state to control re-entry and repetition.

OperationsRefresh, monitor, reconcile, recover

Build freshness and failure awareness into recurring execution.

→

The important design question: what changes between runs, and how do we prove the automation behaves correctly when customer state changes?
